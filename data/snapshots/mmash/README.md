# MMASH, derived file

`derived.json` is a reduced copy of **MMASH: Multilevel Monitoring of Activity and Sleep in
Healthy People**, version 1.0.0, by Rossi A., Da Pozzo E., Menicagli D., Tremolanti C.,
Priami C., Sirbu A., Clifton D., Martini C. and Morelli D., published on PhysioNet:
https://doi.org/10.13026/cerq-fc86

It keeps, per subject: the hourly mean heart rate, the sleep periods reported by the dataset,
and per-minute activity and heart rate during the longest sleep period. It is produced by
`tools/ingest/mmash.mjs` from the original `MMASH.zip`
(SHA-256 `ef91ee1b36b5849a6366b1918de82686ab278915d33c7f62133f07f741f70726`).
